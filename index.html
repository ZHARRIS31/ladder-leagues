Rules

  1. The leagues. Competitive Mixed (singles), Competitive Ladies (singles), Social (singles), Doubles (men's, ladies' or mixed pairs, choose your own partner) and Walking Tennis Doubles. If you play in either competitive league you can't also play in the Social league.
  2. Season. Challenges run from 2 November 2026 to 31 March 2027. Whoever is top of each ladder at the end of 31 March is that league's champion.
  3. Availability. Log any holiday or injury under My ladder. While you're away nobody can challenge you, and the standings show who is free for the dates you pick.
  4. Challenging. You can challenge anyone up to three places above you who is available. Each player or pair can be in only one match at a time.
  5. Wild card. Every player or pair gets one wild card per league per season, to challenge anyone above them however far up.
  6. 72 hours to reply. Once challenged, you must accept within 72 hours. If you don't, the challenger gets the win automatically. After accepting, message the challenger on WhatsApp to agree a date, then enter it in the app.
  7. Venue and booking. Play at Menheniot or Liskeard, whichever you both prefer, and book the court as soon as the date is agreed.
  8. Changing or cancelling. The player challenged enters the agreed date. After that, either player can change the date, time or venue, or cancel the match, under My ladder, and the other player gets an email. A cancelled match doesn't count and nobody moves. A wild card used for it is given back. Before a challenge is accepted, only the challenger can withdraw it.
  9. Format: Fast4, best of three sets. Sets are first to four games. At 3–3 play a tie-break to 5 points, with no need to win by two (5–4 wins). The first deuce of a game is played as normal deuce. At the second deuce the next point wins the game (sudden death), and the receiver chooses which side the server serves from.
  10. Moving up. If the challenger wins, they take the defender's place and everyone in between drops one. If the defender wins, nobody moves and the challenger can't challenge the same opponent again for 14 days.
  11. Scores. Only the challenger enters the score, so results aren't entered twice.
